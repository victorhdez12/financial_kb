# Inflation

## Definition
Sustained increase in the general price level. Specify the measure, frequency, geography and vintage when using a quantitative claim.

## Mechanisms to track
- Demand-side pressure
- Supply shocks
- Money/credit growth
- Wage-price dynamics
- Fiscal/monetary interactions
- Inflation expectations

## Historical episodes
- 1810s / post-war monetary episodes
- 1890s / deflationary period
- 1914-1920 inflation
- 1930s deflation
- 1970s inflation
- 2021-2023 inflation
