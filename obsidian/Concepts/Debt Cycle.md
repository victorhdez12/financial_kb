# Debt Cycle

Use as a causal hypothesis, not as an automatic truth.

## State variables
- Debt / GDP
- Debt service / income
- Credit growth
- Real rates
- Nominal growth
- Asset prices
- Fiscal capacity
- Currency regime

## Questions
1. Is credit expanding or contracting?
2. Is debt service becoming restrictive?
3. Is monetary policy tightening or easing relative to inflation?
4. Is fiscal policy offsetting or amplifying the cycle?
5. What historical episodes are comparable?
