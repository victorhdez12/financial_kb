# Financial & Macro Knowledge Engine

## Purpose

Build a source-grounded macro/market knowledge system covering roughly 1800-present.

## Core dimensions

- Macro: growth, inflation, unemployment, productivity, trade
- Money: money supply, credit, liquidity, central-bank policy
- Fiscal: debt, deficits, taxation, spending, sovereign risk
- Markets: equities, rates, credit, FX, commodities, gold
- Cycles: business, credit, debt, liquidity, valuation
- Institutions: central banks, governments, financial regulators
- Regimes: inflationary, deflationary, reflation, stagflation, crisis
- Investors/frameworks: Dalio, Marks, Buffett, Fisher, Keynes, Minsky, etc.

## Rules

1. Every non-trivial claim should have a source.
2. Keep contradictory claims; do not silently overwrite them.
3. Separate facts, interpretations, hypotheses and forecasts.
4. Historical analogies must list both similarities and differences.
5. Store the data series outside Neo4j; store metadata and relationships in Neo4j.
